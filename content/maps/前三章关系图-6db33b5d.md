- Compactness and Total Boundedness → Weak Topologies and Dual Pairings: use metric compactness on a weak ball
- Algebras, Lattices, and Uniform Approximation → Trigonometric Density and L2 Fourier Expansion: uniform density gives Hilbert density
- Products and Compactness → Weak Topologies and Dual Pairings: finite coordinates plus a norm bound
- Nets and General Convergence → From Metric Sequences to Topological Nets: why sequences may fail
- Compactness and Total Boundedness → From Compactness to Uniform Control: local to uniform estimates
- Nearest Points in Convex Sets → From Algebraic Projections to Hilbert Best Approximation: existence and chosen complement
- Trigonometric Density and L2 Fourier Expansion → From Uniform Approximation to Fourier Expansions: distinguish convergence claims
- Weak Topologies and Dual Pairings → From Coordinate Tests to Weak Compactness: coordinate tests and their boundary
