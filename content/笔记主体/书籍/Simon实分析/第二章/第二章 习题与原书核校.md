## Source checks and local corrections

The following checks concern the local 2015 PDF. “Visually confirmed” means the printed page was inspected, not merely the extracted text. The corrected mathematics below is justified independently. This is a targeted check, not a claim to have checked every printed formula in the chapter.

### SC-2-01 — Bernstein weights at endpoints

Visually confirmed, p.79, (2.4.9): the printed strict positivity must be nonnegativity for $x\in[0,1]$. At $x=0$, all weights except $j=0$ vanish, and at $x=1$ all except $j=n$ vanish. The valid identity is $w_{n,j}(x)\ge0$ and $\sum_jw_{n,j}(x)=1$. See PDF 100（来源 PDF，第 100 页；原文件未公开） and [[笔记主体/书籍/Simon实分析/第二章/知识/Positive Approximation and Bernstein Polynomials|the corrected approximation statement]].

### SC-2-02 — Derivative coefficients in Exercise 8

Visually confirmed, p.87, (2.4.47)–(2.4.48): the sums require the binomial coefficients. For $1\le\ell\le n$, the correct identity is
$$
(B_nf)^{(\ell)}(x)=\frac{n!}{(n-\ell)!}\sum_{j=0}^{n-\ell}
\binom{n-\ell}{j}\bigl(\Delta_{1/n}^{\ell}f\bigr)(j/n)
x^j(1-x)^{n-\ell-j}.
$$
For $\ell=1$, this includes $\binom{n-1}{j}$. The coefficients follow by differentiating the Bernstein basis and collecting adjacent terms. This corrects the identity used by the exercise without filling in its remaining convergence and convexity arguments. PDF 108（来源 PDF，第 108 页；原文件未公开）.

### SC-2-03 — The fixed-point conclusion in Exercise 9(c)

Visually confirmed, p.87: the asserted conclusion that $B_nf=f$ forces $f$ to be constant is false, since $B_n(x)=x$. The exercise must permit affine functions. This entry records the direct counterexample, leaving the corrected classification task unsolved here. PDF 108（来源 PDF，第 108 页；原文件未公开）.

### SC-2-04 — The partition denominator and ambient domain

Visually confirmed, pp.71–72: the proof introduces functions later used on the ambient space $Y$, so their domain must be $Y$. The lower bound for $q=\sum f_{x_k}$ is available on the finite union of the chosen neighborhoods, in particular on the compact target $X$, not on every point of $Y$. The printed quotient must be defined as zero where $q=0$ and its continuity justified from the auxiliary support condition. The [[笔记主体/书籍/Simon实分析/第二章/知识/Semicontinuity, Extrema, and Continuous Partitions#THM — Continuous finite partitions on a compact target|supplementary proof]] instead uses the everywhere positive denominator $\max(q,1/2)$; on the compact target this leaves the desired normalization unchanged. PDF 92（来源 PDF，第 92 页；原文件未公开） · PDF 93（来源 PDF，第 93 页；原文件未公开）.

### SC-2-05 — Hilbert cube open sets

Visually confirmed, §2.2, Problem 6(b), p.63: finite-coordinate cylinders form a base; general open sets are unions of these cylinders. A single cylinder does not represent every open set, contrary to the printed wording. The [[笔记主体/书籍/Simon实分析/第二章/知识/Metrization and the Hilbert Cube|cube page]] uses the base statement. PDF 84（来源 PDF，第 84 页；原文件未公开）.

### SC-2-06 — Uniform convergence needs a domain hypothesis

Visually confirmed, §2.3, Problem 1(b), p.76: the printed word “uniformly” is too strong under the stated hypotheses. Convergence on a dense subset together with uniform equicontinuity and a complete codomain gives pointwise convergence, and uniform convergence on each compact subset. Global uniform convergence follows when the domain is compact, as required later in part (e). The translating tent functions on the [[笔记主体/书籍/Simon实分析/第二章/知识/Equicontinuity and Compact Families#Examples and boundary conditions|equicontinuity page]] show why the distinction is necessary. PDF 97（来源 PDF，第 97 页；原文件未公开）.

### SC-2-07 — Extended-real approximation and empty targets

Theorem 2.3.15 allows $f=+\infty$, so weighted local-infimum expressions need a convention or a separate construction when an infimum is infinite. The notebook supplies an alternative real-valued Lipschitz approximation with that case treated explicitly, and does not multiply $0$ by $+\infty$ silently. Minimum-attainment statements are written for a nonempty compact domain. These are proof-completion and hypothesis checks, rather than assertions of a visually established printing error.
