#用途/正式 #类型/研读 #主题/实分析

Barry Simon, *Real Analysis*, AMS, 2015 · §3.8 · pp. 176–183 · PDF 197（来源 PDF，第 197 页；原文件未公开）–204（来源 PDF，第 204 页；原文件未公开）.

The final section constructs new spaces from old ones. Direct sums keep two components separately, while tensors encode joint dependence on two factors. Although many resulting spaces are abstractly unitarily equivalent, their distinguished factorization and operator actions preserve information that an arbitrary Hilbert-space isomorphism need not preserve.

## Build the direct sum first

For $H_1\oplus H_2$, squared norms add. A sequence of pairs is Cauchy exactly when its two component sequences are, so completeness follows immediately from the factors. The union of the two embedded orthonormal bases gives the new basis, and the norm of $A\oplus B$ is the maximum of the two operator norms. [[笔记主体/书籍/Simon实分析/第三章/知识/Hilbert Sums and Tensor Products#Direct sums|The maintained proof]] separates this external construction from an internal decomposition of one space into complementary subspaces.

## The tensor norm must be well-defined

Simon starts with simple tensors represented as antibilinear forms and takes their finite span. For finite sums, multiplying the factors' inner products gives a candidate formula, but different finite representations might in principle give different answers. Evaluating the represented form proves independence of representation; reducing the finitely many factors to orthonormal bases proves positivity. [[笔记主体/书籍/Simon实分析/第三章/知识/Hilbert Sums and Tensor Products#Proof — Well-definedness and positivity|These checks]] are the difficult part of the construction and are written in full.

Only then is the algebraic tensor space completed. Pairing a completion vector with a simple tensor gives a represented form; density proves this representation is injective. Product basis vectors form an orthonormal basis because finite approximations in each factor approximate every simple tensor. The norm formula for $A\otimes B$ then justifies extending its algebraic action to the completion. This sequence repeats [[笔记主体/书籍/Simon实分析/第一章/知识/Cauchy 序列与度量完备化|Chapter 1's completion method]] with additional linear and inner-product structure that must be preserved.

## Symmetry recovers algebraic constructions

Permutations of factors act unitarily. Averaging their actions, with or without signs, gives orthogonal projections onto symmetric and alternating tensors. The adjoint of a permutation action uses the inverse permutation; averaging over inverses makes the projection self-adjoint. [[笔记主体/书籍/Simon实分析/第三章/知识/Hilbert Sums and Tensor Products#Symmetric and antisymmetric tensors|The proof]] fixes a convention and checks the group and projection identities explicitly.

The chosen wedge normalization gives a determinant formula for its inner product. Increasing-index wedges form an orthonormal basis, and the top exterior power in finite dimension is one-dimensional. Multiplicativity of the induced operator recovers multiplicativity of the determinant. The final exercise branch develops symmetric dimensions and the wedge norm inequality.
