## What Belongs Here

An entry needs actual content, precise conditions, traceable provenance, and a clear proof status. It may originate from one source; a second source is not a prerequisite. Its value lies in an independent account and a real reason for reuse. Book outlines, lecture schedules, and unwritten topic names belong with the corresponding writing plan.

Different books or fields may use the same word for different objects. Compare definitions before merging material. Shared methods can connect domains without erasing differences in assumptions. Add fields and local indexes when the accumulated entries make them useful; do not treat one current textbook as the permanent boundary of the backbone.

## Reuse Without Competing Copies

When a result is distilled, retain its original context in the body note and link to that discussion from the backbone. Give a reusable long proof one maintained home; the body can embed or cite it while retaining the local statement, assumptions, motivation, and transitions. The body should remain a readable account after extraction.

> 主体承担连续讲解，主干承担提炼后的复用与检索。两者按引用互相支持；这不是要求先填满主干，才开始写笔记。
