Given $G\curvearrowright X$ with $X$ finite and a field $k$, let $k[X]$ have basis $\{e_x:x\in X\}$. Extend

$$
\rho(g)e_x=e_{g\cdot x}
$$

linearly. The action laws imply $\rho(gh)=\rho(g)\rho(h)$ and $\rho(g^{-1})=\rho(g)^{-1}$.
