For representations $\rho:G\to\operatorname{GL}_k(V)$ and $\sigma:G\to\operatorname{GL}_k(W)$, an intertwiner is a $k$-linear map $T:V\to W$ with

$$
T\rho(g)=\sigma(g)T\qquad(g\in G).
$$

It need not be invertible. An invertible intertwiner is an isomorphism of representations.
