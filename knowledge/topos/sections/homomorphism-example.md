Reduction modulo $3$ gives an additive homomorphism $\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z$:

$$
n\longmapsto[n],\qquad [m+n]=[m]+[n].
$$

Its kernel is $3\mathbb Z$. 不同整数可以有相同像；这不妨碍它保留加法。
