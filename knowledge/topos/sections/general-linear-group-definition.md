For a $k$-vector space $V$, define

$$
\operatorname{GL}_k(V)=\{T:V\to V:T\text{ is linear and invertible}\}.
$$

For $V=k^n$ with its standard basis, this is the matrix group $\operatorname{GL}_n(k)=\{A:\det A\ne0\}$.
