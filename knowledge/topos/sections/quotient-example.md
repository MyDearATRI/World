Since $3\mathbb Z$ is normal in the additive group $\mathbb Z$, the quotient has three elements:

$$
\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z=\{[0],[1],[2]\},\qquad[2]+[2]=[1].
$$

计算只保留除以 $3$ 的余数。
