A left action of a group $G$ on a set $X$ is a map $G\times X\to X$, written $g\cdot x$, with

$$
e\cdot x=x,\qquad(gh)\cdot x=g\cdot(h\cdot x).
$$

Each map $x\mapsto g\cdot x$ is bijective, with inverse given by $g^{-1}$. Equivalently, an action is a homomorphism $G\to\operatorname{Sym}(X)$.
