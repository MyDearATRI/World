For a set $X$, $\operatorname{Sym}(X)$ is the group of all bijections $X\to X$ under composition. A permutation group on $X$ is a subgroup of this group.

$$
S_n=\operatorname{Sym}(\{1,\ldots,n\}),\qquad |S_n|=n!.
$$
