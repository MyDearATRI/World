For a fixed group $G$ and field $k$, consider representations $G\to\operatorname{GL}_k(V)$ and intertwiners between them. Questions include finding invariant subspaces and comparing representations up to invertible intertwiners.

$$
\operatorname{Hom}_G(V,W)=\{T\text{ linear}:T\rho(g)=\sigma(g)T\text{ for all }g\}.
$$
