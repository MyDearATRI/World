For groups $G,H$, a homomorphism is a map $\varphi:G\to H$ such that

$$
\varphi(gh)=\varphi(g)\varphi(h)\qquad(g,h\in G).
$$

It follows that $\varphi(e_G)=e_H$ and $\varphi(g^{-1})=\varphi(g)^{-1}$. Injectivity is not required.
