For $S_3$ acting on three points, $\rho((123))e_1=e_2$, $\rho((123))e_2=e_3$, and $\rho((123))e_3=e_1$. Over $\mathbb C$,

$$
\chi_\rho(\sigma)=|\{i:\sigma(i)=i\}|.
$$

对角线上恰好在固定的基向量处出现 $1$。
