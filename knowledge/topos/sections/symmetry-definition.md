Fix a structure on a set $X$. Its symmetries are the bijections $X\to X$ preserving that structure, with structure-preserving inverses. Their operation is composition.

$$
\operatorname{Aut}(X)\subseteq\operatorname{Sym}(X).
$$

Here $\operatorname{Sym}(X)$ means all permutations of the underlying set. For a metric structure, the relevant symmetries are bijective isometries.
