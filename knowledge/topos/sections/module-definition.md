For a unital $k$-algebra $A$, a left $A$-module is a $k$-vector space $V$ with a bilinear action satisfying

$$
(ab)v=a(bv),\qquad1_Av=v.
$$

A representation $\rho:G\to\operatorname{GL}_k(V)$ extends uniquely to a $k[G]$-module by $(\sum_g a_g[g])v=\sum_g a_g\rho(g)v$. Conversely, restriction to $[g]$ recovers $\rho$.
