For bijections $f,g,h:X\to X$, evaluate both bracketings at an arbitrary $x$:

$$
((f\circ g)\circ h)(x)=f(g(h(x)))=(f\circ(g\circ h))(x).
$$

Thus function composition is associative. This verifies the associativity axiom for symmetry transformations, without asserting that $f\circ g=g\circ f$.

这是一个完整的逐点检验；下一层把它放回三角形的具体变换。
