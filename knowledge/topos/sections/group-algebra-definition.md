For a field $k$ and a group $G$, the algebra $k[G]$ has basis $\{[g]:g\in G\}$. Its elements are finite sums, with

$$
\left(\sum_g a_g[g]\right)\left(\sum_h b_h[h]\right)=\sum_{g,h}a_gb_h[gh].
$$

The unit is $[e]$. Even when $G$ is infinite, only finitely many coefficients in each element are nonzero.
