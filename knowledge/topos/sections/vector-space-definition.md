A $k$-vector space is an abelian group $(V,+)$ with scalar multiplication $k\times V\to V$ such that, for $a,b\in k$ and $u,v\in V$,

$$
a(u+v)=au+av,\quad(a+b)v=av+bv,\quad(ab)v=a(bv),\quad1v=v.
$$

All operations have values in $V$.
