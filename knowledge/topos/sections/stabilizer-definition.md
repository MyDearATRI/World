For $G\curvearrowright X$ and $x\in X$, the stabilizer is the subgroup

$$
G_x=\{g\in G:g\cdot x=x\}\leq G.
$$

There is a bijection of $G$-sets $G/G_x\to G\cdot x$, $gG_x\mapsto g\cdot x$. Here $G/G_x$ is a coset set; $G_x$ need not be normal.
