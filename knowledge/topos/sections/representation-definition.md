Let $k$ be a field and $V$ a $k$-vector space. A representation of $G$ on $V$ is a group homomorphism

$$
\rho:G\to\operatorname{GL}_k(V),\qquad \rho(gh)=\rho(g)\rho(h).
$$

Writing $g\cdot v=\rho(g)v$ gives a linear action. Conversely every linear group action determines such a homomorphism.

这里用到的[向量空间](section:vector-space-definition)和[线性映射](section:linear-map-definition)可以在原处继续展开；同一数据也可用[模](concept:module)描述。
