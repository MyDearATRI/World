Compare the trivial representation of $C_3$ on $\mathbb C$ with $r\mapsto\zeta=e^{2\pi i/3}$. An intertwiner $T:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ must satisfy

$$
T=\zeta T.
$$

Since $\zeta\ne1$, this forces $T=0$. 线性映射存在很多，但与这两个作用相容的只有零映射。
