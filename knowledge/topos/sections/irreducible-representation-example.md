Every one-dimensional representation is irreducible: a one-dimensional vector space has only the subspaces $0$ and itself. Thus $r\mapsto e^{2\pi i/3}$ defines an irreducible complex representation of $C_3$.

这项结论不需要先使用 Maschke 定理。
