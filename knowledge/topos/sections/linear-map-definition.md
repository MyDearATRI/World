For vector spaces $V,W$ over the same field $k$, a map $T:V\to W$ is $k$-linear when

$$
T(au+bv)=aT(u)+bT(v)\qquad(a,b\in k;\ u,v\in V).
$$

Linearity alone does not imply invertibility.
