For a finite group over $\mathbb C$, finite-dimensional representations decompose into direct sums of irreducible representations. This is a consequence of Maschke's theorem.

这个结论在模特征整除群阶时不能直接照搬。这里仅陈述有明确条件的结果，不把导航中的聚类当成证明。
