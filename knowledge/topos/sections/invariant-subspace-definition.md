For a representation $\rho$ on $V$, a subspace $W\leq V$ is invariant when

$$
\rho(g)W\subseteq W\qquad\text{for every }g\in G.
$$

Because $g^{-1}$ also acts, equality holds. Restricting the operators to $W$ gives a subrepresentation.
