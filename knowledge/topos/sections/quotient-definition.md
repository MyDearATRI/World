Let $N\trianglelefteq G$ be a normal subgroup. The left cosets form a group with multiplication

$$
(gN)(hN)=(gh)N.
$$

Normality makes this independent of representatives. The map $q:G\to G/N$, $q(g)=gN$, is a surjective homomorphism with kernel $N$.
