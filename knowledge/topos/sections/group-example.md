Let $r$ rotate an equilateral triangle by $120^\circ$ and let $s$ reflect it across a symmetry axis. Composition gives

$$
r^3=e,\qquad s^2=e,\qquad srs=r^{-1}.
$$

The six transformations $e,r,r^2,s,rs,r^2s$ form its symmetry group. Products act from right to left.

这里的乘法是变换的复合；反射与旋转通常不能交换。
