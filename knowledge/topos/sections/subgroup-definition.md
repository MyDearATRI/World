A subset $H\subseteq G$ is a subgroup if it contains $e$ and is closed under products and inverses:

$$
a,b\in H\Longrightarrow ab\in H,\quad a^{-1}\in H.
$$

Associativity is inherited from $G$. The identity and inverses agree with those in $G$.
