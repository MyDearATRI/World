For the cyclic group $C_3=\langle r\mid r^3=e\rangle$, let $V=\mathbb R^2$ and

$$
\rho(r)=\begin{pmatrix}-\tfrac12&-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\\[2pt]\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}&-\tfrac12\end{pmatrix}.
$$

This rotates vectors by $120^\circ$. Since $\rho(r)^3=I$, the formula defines a representation. 定义域是抽象群，作用对象是向量。
