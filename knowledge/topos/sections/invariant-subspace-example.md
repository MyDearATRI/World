Let $S_3$ permute the standard basis of $\mathbb C^3$. The line

$$
W=\mathbb C(e_1+e_2+e_3)
$$

is invariant, and every permutation acts as the identity on it. 因为它是非零真子空间，这个三维置换表示可约。
