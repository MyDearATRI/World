A representation $(V,\rho)$ over $k$ is irreducible if $V\ne0$ and its only subspaces invariant under every $\rho(g)$ are

$$
W=0\quad\text{or}\quad W=V.
$$

Equivalently, the corresponding $k[G]$-module is simple. No decomposition theorem is asserted without further hypotheses.
