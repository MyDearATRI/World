Let $G$ be a finite group and $\rho:G\to\operatorname{GL}_{\mathbb C}(V)$ with $\dim_{\mathbb C}V<\infty$. Its character is

$$
\chi_\rho(g)=\operatorname{tr}(\rho(g)),\qquad\chi_\rho(e)=\dim V.
$$

It is constant on conjugacy classes because trace is unchanged by conjugation.
